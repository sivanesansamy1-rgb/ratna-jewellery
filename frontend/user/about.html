Our Story

Three generations of goldsmiths, one promise: jewellery you can trust.

Crafted in Jaipur, worn everywhere

RATNA began as a single workbench in Jaipur's old jewellery quarter, where our founder hand-set stones for local families. Today, that same attention to detail guides every piece we make — from a everyday gold chain to a full bridal set.

Every product is BIS hallmarked for gold purity, and our diamonds are certified for cut, clarity and carat. We photograph and weigh each piece before it leaves our workshop, so what you see online is exactly what arrives at your door.
